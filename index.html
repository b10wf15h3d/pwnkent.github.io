// 01 about

Your local between conferences

BSides and the big conferences are brilliant, but they only come round a few times a year. Pwn Kent is a relaxed monthly get-together in a Tunbridge Wells pub, where Kent's security community can meet, swap stories and learn from each other.

Meet

Put faces to names and get to know the people working in security across Kent and down the line.

Learn

Short talks, demos and war stories from the community, from first-timers to seasoned pros.

Socialise

No sales pitches, no pressure. Just good conversation over a drink, alcoholic or not.

// 05 code of conduct

Be excellent to each other

Pwn Kent is for everyone, whatever your background, experience or level. We expect everyone who comes along to:

  • Be welcoming, respectful and patient, especially with newcomers.
  • Not harass, intimidate or discriminate against anyone, in person or online.
  • Keep it legal and ethical: no hacking of the venue, attendees or their devices, and no sharing of others' confidential information.
  • Respect "no photos" requests and Chatham House Rule when a speaker asks for it.
  • Drink responsibly, or not at all. Nobody needs to explain why they're on soft drinks.

If something makes you uncomfortable, speak to the organiser on the night or get in touch. Anyone who breaks these rules may be asked to leave.

// 06 privacy

Privacy notice

This website sets no cookies. We use GoatCounter, a privacy-friendly analytics service, to count page views. It doesn't use cookies, track you across sites or collect personal data.

What we collect

If you get in touch to register your interest, we keep your name, how to contact you and anything you choose to share, such as your preferred night or interest in giving a talk.

Why we collect it

Only to tell you about Pwn Kent meetups and to plan them. We rely on your consent, which you can withdraw at any time.

Who handles it

For now, people register by messaging the organiser on LinkedIn, which is covered by LinkedIn's own privacy policy. We may later use a sign-up form or email service, and will update this notice if we do. We never sell or share your details with anyone else.

How long we keep it

Until you ask us to remove you, or Pwn Kent stops running.

Your rights

Under UK GDPR you can ask to see, correct or delete your data, or withdraw your consent. To do so, get in touch. You can also complain to the Information Commissioner's Office at ico.org.uk.
